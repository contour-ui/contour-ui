Dialog is the [Headless Dialog](/docs/headless/dialog) with the look of Kit: the scrim of the
overlay over the page, and a raised panel with a border, a shadow and the corners of a panel. It
grows in when it opens and fades out when it closes. While it is open, focus stays inside it, the
page does not scroll and the rest of it is out of reach; Escape, a press on the scrim, the x and
the other close triggers close it, and focus goes back to the trigger.

## The parts

- **The overlay.** `Portal` renders the backdrop and the positioner at the end of the page, above
  everything else. The positioner holds the content in the middle of the viewport, with a margin
  all round, and lets presses around it through to the backdrop.
- **The trigger** has no look of its own: pass a `Button` with `asChild`.
- **The x.** `Dialog.CloseTrigger` is a ghost x button, named "Close", in the top end corner of
  the content, 24 px square: the smallest target WCAG 2.2 asks for. The header and a title right in
  the content make room for it. Pass another `label` to say what closes, such as "Close the
  export".
- **Header, body, footer.** The header stacks the title and the description. The body takes the
  rest of the height, and scrolls between the header and the footer when the dialog is taller than
  the viewport. The footer lays out its actions in a row, at the end. Every part is optional: a
  title and a field right in the content work too.
- **Other ways to close.** With `asChild`, `Dialog.CloseTrigger` makes a button of yours close the
  dialog, keeping its look and place, as the Cancel button above.

## Placement and size

`placement` on the root puts the dialog in the middle of the viewport (`center`, the default), or
near its top (`top`), as a search that grows down as its results change. `size` sets how wide the
dialog can grow: `sm` (400 px) for a question, `md` (560 px, the default) for a form or a search,
`lg` (800 px) for more content. It never grows wider than the viewport.

```tsx
<Dialog.Root placement="top" size="md">
  …
</Dialog.Root>
```

## Open and close

The dialog keeps its own state; `defaultOpen` opens it from the start. To keep it yourself, pass
`open` and `onOpenChange`, which reports each request to open or close as `{ open }`, and the
dialog changes when you pass the new `open`:

```tsx
const [open, setOpen] = useState(false);

<Dialog.Root open={open} onOpenChange={({ open }) => setOpen(open)}>
  …
</Dialog.Root>;
```

`closeOnEscape` and `closeOnInteractOutside` turn off Escape and the press on the scrim. A
dialog with `modal={false}` leaves the page within reach: render no backdrop for it. Focus goes to
the first element of the dialog when it opens, or to `initialFocusEl()`, and back to the trigger,
or to `finalFocusEl()`, when it closes. Every option of the
[Headless Dialog](/docs/headless/dialog#props) works the same here.

## Alert dialogs

`role="alertdialog"` asks for an answer. Leave out the x, and put the safe answer first: the
dialog focuses its first close trigger, and a press on the scrim does not close it.

```tsx
<Dialog.Root role="alertdialog" size="sm">
  <Dialog.Trigger asChild>
    <Button variant="outline">Delete the take</Button>
  </Dialog.Trigger>
  <Portal>
    <Dialog.Backdrop />
    <Dialog.Positioner>
      <Dialog.Content>
        <Dialog.Header>
          <Dialog.Title>Delete the take?</Dialog.Title>
          <Dialog.Description>It is gone for good.</Dialog.Description>
        </Dialog.Header>
        <Dialog.Footer>
          <Dialog.CloseTrigger asChild>
            <Button variant="outline">Keep it</Button>
          </Dialog.CloseTrigger>
          <Dialog.CloseTrigger asChild>
            <Button onClick={deleteTake}>Delete</Button>
          </Dialog.CloseTrigger>
        </Dialog.Footer>
      </Dialog.Content>
    </Dialog.Positioner>
  </Portal>
</Dialog.Root>
```

## Scrolling

The dialog is never taller than the viewport: a long `Dialog.Body` scrolls, from edge to edge of
the dialog, while the header and the footer stay in view. It opens scrolled to the element focus
moves to, such as `initialFocusEl()` below the fold. A body that holds only text takes no
focus by itself, so give it `tabIndex={0}`: keyboard users can then focus it and scroll it with
the arrow keys.

## Anatomy

| Part                  | Element  | Class                           | Attributes                                                                                        |
| --------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Dialog.Root`         | none     | —                               | —                                                                                                 |
| `Dialog.Trigger`      | `button` | `contour-dialog__trigger`       | `data-scope="dialog" data-part="trigger"`, `aria-haspopup`, `aria-expanded`, `data-state`         |
| `Dialog.Backdrop`     | `div`    | `contour-dialog__backdrop`      | `data-part="backdrop"`, `data-state`                                                              |
| `Dialog.Positioner`   | `div`    | `contour-dialog__positioner`    | `data-part="positioner"`, `data-state`, `data-placement`                                          |
| `Dialog.Content`      | `div`    | `contour-dialog__content`       | `data-part="content"`, `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, `data-state`, `data-placement`, `data-size` |
| `Dialog.CloseTrigger` | `button` | `contour-dialog__close-trigger` | `data-part="close-trigger"`; with `asChild`, the class of your element                            |
| `Dialog.Header`       | `div`    | `contour-dialog__header`        | `data-part="header"`                                                                              |
| `Dialog.Body`         | `div`    | `contour-dialog__body`          | `data-part="body"`                                                                                |
| `Dialog.Footer`       | `div`    | `contour-dialog__footer`        | `data-part="footer"`                                                                              |
| `Dialog.Title`        | `h2`     | `contour-dialog__title`         | `data-part="title"`, `id`                                                                         |
| `Dialog.Description`  | `p`      | `contour-dialog__description`   | `data-part="description"`, `id`                                                                   |

Every part carries `data-scope="dialog"`; `data-state` is `"open"` or `"closed"`. The closed
overlay stays mounted, `hidden`, unless `unmountOnExit`; while it fades out it is `inert`. The
content, the positioner and the backdrop get `--layer-index` while the dialog is open: their place
among the open overlays, so a dialog opened from another one goes above it. The
[Headless Dialog](/docs/headless/dialog#anatomy) lists every attribute.

## Props

`Dialog.Root` takes every prop of the [Headless Dialog](/docs/headless/dialog#props) (`open`,
`defaultOpen`, `onOpenChange`, `modal`, `closeOnEscape`, `closeOnInteractOutside`, `role`,
`initialFocusEl`, `finalFocusEl`, `restoreFocus`, `lazyMount`, `unmountOnExit`…), and:

| Prop        | Values                 | Default    |
| ----------- | ---------------------- | ---------- |
| `placement` | `'center'`, `'top'`    | `'center'` |
| `size`      | `'sm'`, `'md'`, `'lg'` | `'md'`     |

`Dialog.CloseTrigger` takes the props of [`CloseButton`](/docs/kit/button), `label` (`'Close'`),
`variant` (`'ghost'`) and `size` (`'md'`) among them, or, with `asChild`, renders its child. Every
other part takes `asChild` and the props of its element, such as `className`, `style` and `ref`;
`className` is merged with the part's class.

## Keyboard

| Key                  | Action                                                           |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Enter`, `Space`     | On the trigger: opens the dialog. On a close trigger: closes it  |
| `Tab`, `Shift`+`Tab` | Move focus to the next or the previous element, round the dialog |
| `Escape`             | Closes the dialog                                                |

## Accessibility

- The content has the `dialog` role (or `alertdialog`), `aria-modal`, and is named by its title
  and described by its description: screen readers announce both as focus moves in. A dialog
  without a title needs an `aria-label`.
- The x is named "Close". Name it after what closes when the page has several things that do.
- While the dialog is open, the rest of the page is `inert`: out of reach of the pointer, the
  keyboard and screen readers.
- The shadow is decoration: the raised background and the border set the dialog apart without it.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, the dialog fades in and out without growing.
