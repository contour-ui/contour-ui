A drawer is a [Dialog](/docs/kit/dialog) that slides in from an edge of the screen: the same
parts on the [Headless Dialog](/docs/headless/dialog), with the same behavior. While it is open,
focus stays inside it, the page does not scroll and the rest of it is out of reach; Escape, a press
on the scrim, the x and the other close triggers close it, and focus goes back to the trigger. It
is a panel on the surface color, with a border on its inner side and a shadow, and it slides back
out when it closes.

## Placement

`placement` on the root is the edge the drawer slides in from. `start`, the default, and `end` follow
the direction of the text: in a left-to-right page they are the left and the right edge, and in a
right-to-left page the other way round. From a side, the drawer is as high as the screen; from
`top` or `bottom`, it is as wide as the screen and as high as its content.

```tsx
<Drawer.Root placement="end">…</Drawer.Root>
```

## Size

`size` sets how far the drawer reaches in: from a side, its width, `sm` (320 px, the default),
`md` (448 px) or `lg` (640 px); from the top or the bottom, its greatest height. `full` covers the
screen. The drawer is never larger than the screen.

## The parts

They are the parts of [Dialog](/docs/kit/dialog#the-parts), with their own classes:
`Drawer.CloseTrigger` is the x in the top end corner, or, with `asChild`, a button of yours that
closes the drawer; the header stacks the title and the description; the body takes the rest of the
height and scrolls, from edge to edge of the drawer, opening scrolled to the element focus moves
to; the footer sits at the far end and lays out its actions in a row. Put the backdrop and the
positioner in a `Portal`.

## Open and close

Every option of the [Headless Dialog](/docs/headless/dialog#props) works the same here:
`defaultOpen`, `open` and `onOpenChange` to keep the state yourself, `closeOnEscape`,
`closeOnInteractOutside`, `initialFocusEl`, `finalFocusEl`. Close a navigation drawer once a
link is followed: keep `open` in your state, and set it to `false` when the page changes.

```tsx
const [open, setOpen] = useState(false);
const { pathname } = useLocation();
useEffect(() => {
  setOpen(false);
}, [pathname]);

<Drawer.Root open={open} onOpenChange={({ open }) => setOpen(open)}>
  …
</Drawer.Root>;
```

## Anatomy

| Part                  | Element  | Class                           | Attributes                                                                                        |
| --------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Drawer.Root`         | none     | —                               | —                                                                                                 |
| `Drawer.Trigger`      | `button` | `contour-drawer__trigger`       | `data-scope="dialog" data-part="trigger"`, `aria-haspopup`, `aria-expanded`, `data-state`         |
| `Drawer.Backdrop`     | `div`    | `contour-drawer__backdrop`      | `data-part="backdrop"`, `data-state`                                                              |
| `Drawer.Positioner`   | `div`    | `contour-drawer__positioner`    | `data-part="positioner"`, `data-state`, `data-placement`                                          |
| `Drawer.Content`      | `div`    | `contour-drawer__content`       | `data-part="content"`, `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, `data-state`, `data-placement`, `data-size` |
| `Drawer.CloseTrigger` | `button` | `contour-drawer__close-trigger` | `data-part="close-trigger"`; with `asChild`, the class of your element                            |
| `Drawer.Header`       | `div`    | `contour-drawer__header`        | `data-part="header"`                                                                              |
| `Drawer.Body`         | `div`    | `contour-drawer__body`          | `data-part="body"`                                                                                |
| `Drawer.Footer`       | `div`    | `contour-drawer__footer`        | `data-part="footer"`                                                                              |
| `Drawer.Title`        | `h2`     | `contour-drawer__title`         | `data-part="title"`, `id`                                                                         |
| `Drawer.Description`  | `p`      | `contour-drawer__description`   | `data-part="description"`, `id`                                                                   |

A drawer is a dialog: every part carries `data-scope="dialog"`. Style a drawer by its classes.

## Props

`Drawer.Root` takes every prop of the [Headless Dialog](/docs/headless/dialog#props), and:

| Prop        | Values                                  | Default   |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------- | --------- |
| `placement` | `'start'`, `'end'`, `'top'`, `'bottom'` | `'start'` |
| `size`      | `'sm'`, `'md'`, `'lg'`, `'full'`        | `'sm'`    |

`Drawer.CloseTrigger` takes the props of [`CloseButton`](/docs/kit/button), `size` (`'md'`, a
24 px target) among them, or, with `asChild`, renders its child. Every other part takes `asChild`
and the props of its element; `className` is merged with the part's class.

## Keyboard

| Key                  | Action                                                           |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Enter`, `Space`     | On the trigger: opens the drawer. On a close trigger: closes it  |
| `Tab`, `Shift`+`Tab` | Move focus to the next or the previous element, round the drawer |
| `Escape`             | Closes the drawer                                                |

## Accessibility

- The content is a modal dialog, named by its title: screen readers announce it as focus moves
  in, and the rest of the page is `inert` while it is open.
- Focus goes back to the trigger, such as the menu button, when the drawer closes.
- `start` and `end` follow the direction of the text, so the drawer slides in from the side
  readers of the page expect.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, the drawer fades in and out without sliding.

| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| `--ct-color-overlay-scrim`                                          | the backdrop                                          |
| `--ct-color-bg-surface`                                             | the background of the content                         |
| `--ct-color-border-default`, `--ct-border-width-default`            | the border on the inner side                          |
| `--ct-shadow-overlay`                                               | the shadow of the content                             |

| `--ct-space-panel-inset`                                            | the padding of the content and the gap of its parts   |

| `--ct-text-heading`, `--ct-color-fg-default`                        | the title                                             |
| `--ct-text-body`, `--ct-color-fg-muted`                             | the description; other content is `fg-default`        |
| `--ct-size-control-md`                                              | the x, and the room the header makes for it           |
| `--ct-layer-overlay`                                                | the `z-index` of the overlay, plus `--layer-index`    |
| `--ct-motion-enter`, `--ct-motion-exit`                             | the slide of the content and the fade of the backdrop |
