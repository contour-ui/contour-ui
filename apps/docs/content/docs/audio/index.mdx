Audio is the section of the controls of a plugin interface: knobs, faders, meters, spectrum
displays and value readouts. They ship in `@contour-ui/kit`, next to the everyday components, and
follow the same [themes](/docs/guide/themes) and [tokens](/docs/guide/styling). None ships yet:
they are the next components to come, and their pages will appear here.

## Setup

They need nothing beyond Kit's setup: install `@contour-ui/kit` and load its stylesheet, as
[Installation](/docs/guide/installation) shows.

## Conventions

- **Readouts** use the `value` text style: monospace, so numbers do not shift while the value
  changes.
- **Customizing** works as for every Kit component: each control has a class,
  `contour-<component>`, and your `className` is merged in.
