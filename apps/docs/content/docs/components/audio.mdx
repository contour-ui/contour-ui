Audio is the section of the controls of a plugin interface: knobs, faders, meters, spectrum
displays and value readouts. They ship in `@contour-ui/react`, next to the everyday components, and
follow the same [themes](/docs/guide/themes) and [tokens](/docs/guide/styling). Knob is the first
of them; faders, meters and the others come next, and their pages will appear here.

## Setup

They need no setup of their own: install `@contour-ui/react` and load its stylesheet, as
[Installation](/docs/guide/installation) shows.

## Conventions

- **Readouts** use the `value` text style: monospace, so numbers do not shift while the value
  changes. The formatters of `@contour-ui/react` write them as plugins show them, without `Intl`:
  `formatDecibels`, `formatFrequency`, `formatDuration`, `formatPercent` and `formatNumber`.
- **Scales** spread a range over the travel of a control: `'linear'`, `'log'` for frequencies and
  times, `powerScale(exponent)` for a curve in between, or a `Scale` of your own.
- **Gestures.** Each change the user makes has a start and an end (`onValueChangeStart`,
  `onValueChangeEnd`), so a plugin can record it as one step of the host's automation.
- **Customizing** works as for the other [components](/docs/components#customizing): each control has a
  class, `contour-<component>`, and your `className` is merged in.
