Add text styles for site pages such as a home page: `text.hero` (60 px, semibold, line height 1.05, letter spacing −1 px), the hero title; `text.lead` (16 px, regular, line height 1.6), the paragraph under it; and `text.nav` (15 px, medium, line height 1.2), the navigation links of a site header. Each comes as `--ct-text-<style>` (the `font` shorthand) with its `-font-family`, `-font-size`, `-font-weight`, `-line-height` and `-letter-spacing` properties, and in `vars.text`.
