Add `Popover`: `Root`, `Trigger`, `Anchor`, `Positioner`, `Content`, `Title`, `Description` and `CloseTrigger` parts on the popover machine of `@contour-ui/core`. The content is a `dialog` named by its title, placed next to the trigger (or the anchor) on Floating UI, below at the start edge by default, flipping and sliding to stay in view; put the positioner in a `Portal` to lift it out of the trigger's parents, or tell a popover rendered in place with `portalled={false}`. Escape, a press outside or focus moving out closes the top-most popover (`closeOnEscape`, `closeOnInteractOutside`), and a popover opened from inside another one closes with it. Opening moves focus into the content (`autoFocus`, `initialFocusEl`), closing moves it back to the trigger (`restoreFocus`, `finalFocusEl`) unless it has moved on to something outside, and the content keeps its place in the Tab order after the trigger. A `modal` popover traps focus, locks the scroll and makes the rest of the page inert. `open` / `defaultOpen` / `onOpenChange` work as in the other components; `lazyMount`, `unmountOnExit` and `onExitComplete` work as in `Presence`: the content waits for its exit animation before it hides. `usePopover` and `usePopoverContext` give the same API to parts of your own.
