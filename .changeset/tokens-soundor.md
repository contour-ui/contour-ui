Add `@contour-ui/tokens/soundor`: the values of every semantic token per theme as Soundor styles take them. Colors are hex (8 digits for a translucent role), lengths and durations plain numbers in px and ms, text styles the style properties of a Soundor `Text` (one font family, the line height in px), and transitions carry the named easing closest to their curve. Shadows have no Soundor equivalent and are left out.
