Add `Button`, `IconButton` and `CloseButton`. `Button` comes in the `solid`, `subtle`, `outline` and `ghost` variants and three sizes; with `asChild` it renders a link, and `disabled` then sets `aria-disabled` and cancels clicks. `IconButton` is a square button with only an icon and a required `label`; `CloseButton` is a ghost icon button with an x, labeled "Close".
