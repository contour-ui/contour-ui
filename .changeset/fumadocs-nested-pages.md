A page left out of the page tree belongs to its nearest ancestor in it: at `/docs/components/dialog/headless`, a page the tree does not list, `DocsLayout` and `DocsPage` mark `/docs/components/dialog` as current in the sidebar, show its section in the switcher and its neighbours in the previous and next links. A site can list a page once and keep tabs of it, such as a headless version, as pages of their own.
