Add `Tabs` (`Root`, `List`, `Trigger`, `Content`, `Indicator`) on Headless Tabs, in the variants `line` (default), `enclosed` and `subtle` and the sizes `sm`, `md` and `lg`. Every part renders the `data-variant` and `data-size` of its root. The selected trigger is marked by a bar, a raised box or a filled box besides its color; the indicator takes the mark over once measured and slides it between the triggers, without sliding under reduced motion.
