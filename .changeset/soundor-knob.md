Add `Knob`: the rotary control of the web `Knob` in its three sizes (`size`: `sm`, `md`, `lg`, the `size.knob-*` tokens), on `@contour-ui/soundor-headless`, drawn with the token values of the theme; `Knob.Control` draws the track, the range and the thumb when it has no children, and `Knob.Label`, `Knob.Control` and `Knob.ValueText` take a `style` function of the knob. The readouts and scales of value controls are re-exported. Its focus ring is Soundor's outline, as the button's.
