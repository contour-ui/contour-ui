Add positioning of floating elements on `@floating-ui/core`, for any renderer. `computePosition(reference, floating, { ...options, platform })` turns the options (`placement`, `offset`, `flip`, `shift`, `sameWidth`, `fitViewport`, `strategy`) into Floating UI middleware and computes with the platform the adapter passes: the DOM platform of `@floating-ui/dom`, or rectangles from another renderer. The adapter may also pass `compute`, the `computePosition` of the Floating UI package its platform comes from; the DOM passes the one of `@floating-ui/dom`, which keeps its caches during a computation. It returns plain data: the coordinates, the placement after `flip`, the size of the reference and the room around the floating element, its transform origin, and the same values as the CSS variables `--reference-width`, `--reference-height`, `--available-width`, `--available-height` and `--transform-origin`. `shift` keeps the floating element its distance from the edges of the viewport, and `flip` and `fitViewport` count that distance too. `resolvePositioningOptions` fills in the defaults and layers the defaults of a component under the options of its owner.
