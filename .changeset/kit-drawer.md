Add `Drawer`: a dialog that slides in from an edge of the viewport, on the Headless Dialog, with the parts of `Dialog` and their own classes. `placement` is the edge: `start` (the default) and `end`, which follow the direction of the text, `top` or `bottom`; `size` (`sm`, `md`, `lg`, `full`) is how far it reaches in. The content is a panel on the surface color with a border on its inner side and a shadow; its body takes the free height and scrolls, and its footer sits at the far end. It slides in and out, and only fades under reduced motion.
