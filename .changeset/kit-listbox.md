Add `Listbox`, the Headless listbox with the Kit look: the highlighted item on the hover background, a check mark in the accent color on the selected ones (`Listbox.ItemIndicator`, which keeps its room on the others), group labels in muted caption text, an outlined text field for `Listbox.Input`, and muted text for `Listbox.Empty`. The list has no frame of its own and scrolls once it has a height. `createListCollection` is re-exported, so a Listbox needs no other package.
