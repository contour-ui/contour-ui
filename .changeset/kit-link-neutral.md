Add `variant="neutral"` to `Link`, for lists of navigation links such as a site header, an index or a footer: text in `--ct-color-fg-muted` that turns `--ct-color-fg-default` on hover with a `--ct-motion-feedback` color transition, never underlined, with the focus ring of the other variants. Keep it out of running text, where a link must not stand out by color alone. `plain` and `underline` do not change.
