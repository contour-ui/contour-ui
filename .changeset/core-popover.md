Add the popover machine, exported as the `popover` namespace: `popover.machine`, `popover.connect` (`open`, `modal`, `portalled`, `placement`, `setOpen()`, `toggle()`) and its types. `OPEN`, `CLOSE` and `TOGGLE` move the popover between `closed` and `open` and report each change as `onOpenChange({ open })`, never a request for the state it is in. `ESCAPE` and `INTERACT_OUTSIDE`, sent by the platform's `dismiss` effect, close it unless `closeOnEscape` or `closeOnInteractOutside` is turned off. With `open` controlled, requests are only reported and the state follows the prop; otherwise it starts from `defaultOpen`. While open, the machine runs the platform effects `position`, which places the content as `positioning` asks (by default below the anchor at its start edge, 8px away, flipping and sliding to stay in view, no larger than the room it has) and writes the placement it finds to the context, `dismiss`, `trapFocus`, `preventScroll` and `hideOutside`, the last three for a modal popover only, and `keepTabOrder`, which keeps portalled content in the Tab order after its trigger. Opening moves focus into the content when `autoFocus` is set (`setInitialFocus`), but a popover that starts open leaves focus alone; closing moves focus back when `restoreFocus` is set, unless it has moved on to something outside the popover.
